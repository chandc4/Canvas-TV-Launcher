# Privacy Policy — Canvas TV Launcher

Last updated: 2026

Canvas TV Launcher is a free, open-source Android TV launcher. It is designed to be simple, local-first, and privacy-respecting.

## Data collection
Canvas TV Launcher does **not** collect, store, or transmit personal data to remote servers.

## Network use
The app includes an optional local web server for transferring wallpapers and videos from a phone or tablet to the TV. This server:
- runs only while Launcher Settings are open
- listens on the local network (port 8080)
- does not send data to the internet
- is stopped when Settings are closed or the app is backgrounded

## Storage
Uploaded wallpapers and videos are stored in the app's private storage on the TV. The app does not upload your files to any cloud service.

## Permissions
The app may request:
- Internet / network state / Wi-Fi state: used only for the local phone-to-TV transfer feature.
- Storage access is limited to app-private storage where possible.

## Third-party services
Canvas TV Launcher does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Children
The app is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes
This policy may be updated as the app evolves. The latest version will be published with the project.

## Contact
For questions about this policy, open an issue in the project repository.
