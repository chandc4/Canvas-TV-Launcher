# Canvas TV Launcher — Help

## Basic use
- The home screen shows your current wallpaper canvas.
- Press **D-pad Down** to open the All Apps drawer.
- Press **Back** to close overlays.
- The bottom dock shows your pinned apps.

## Pin apps to the dock
1. Press D-pad Down to open All Apps.
2. Select an app.
3. Tap/click it to launch it and pin it to the dock.
4. Use Settings → Apps to hide or reorder apps.

## Change dock size
1. Open Settings (top-right settings button).
2. Go to **Dock & Dim**.
3. Use `-` / `+` to set dock size from 0 to 10.
4. `0` hides the dock.

## Add wallpapers or videos from your phone
1. Open Settings → Transfer.
2. Scan the QR code or open the shown URL on your phone.
3. Upload photos or videos.
4. Photos become wallpapers; videos appear in the Movies shelf.

## Apply or delete wallpapers
- Open Settings → Wallpapers.
- Click a wallpaper to apply it.
- Long-press (or use delete) to remove it.

## Play videos
- Open Settings → Movies.
- Click a video to open it in VLC or Nova Video Player.

## Ambient rotation
- Open Settings → Ambient.
- Choose Off, 15s, 30s, 1m, or 1h.
- Rotation pauses while the screen is off and resumes when you return Home.

## Keep screen on
- Open Settings → Ambient.
- Enable “Keep Screen On (Ambient)” if you want the TV panel to stay lit while Home is visible.

## Set as default launcher
1. Open Settings → System.
2. Use “Set as Default Home”.
3. Or open TV System Settings → Apps → Default apps/Home app and select Canvas TV Launcher.

## Recovery if the launcher fails to start
1. Open TV System Settings.
2. Go to Apps → Default apps (Home app).
3. Select your stock launcher.
4. You can also reboot and switch there.
5. Keep the stock launcher installed.

## Troubleshooting
- **No wallpapers uploaded yet**: use Transfer to add photos.
- **No videos yet**: upload `.mp4` or `.mkv` from your phone.
- **Not enough space**: delete old wallpapers/videos or free storage on the TV.
- **App not in dock**: pin it from All Apps, then increase dock size.
